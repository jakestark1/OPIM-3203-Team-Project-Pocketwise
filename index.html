Pocketwise could not load its files

This usually happens when index.html is opened from inside the ZIP file, or copied away from its folder.

  1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All..., then click Extract.
  2. Open the extracted folder (keep index.html, styles.css and the js folder together).
  3. Double-click index.html.
